At a Glance

Embodied intelligence R&D for underwater environments

The center focuses on underwater robotics, intelligent perception, simulation-based validation, and autonomous systems for complex underwater environments.

Perception and Understanding

Multimodal perception and scene understanding for low-light, dynamic underwater environments.

Autonomy and Control

Embodied decision-making, motion control, and mission planning for underwater robotic systems.

Simulation and Platforms

Simulation, digital twins, and experimental platforms for repeatable algorithm validation.
